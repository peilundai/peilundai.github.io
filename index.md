I am a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Grab in Singapore, where I build production AI systems to solve real-world problems.

Before joining Grab, I worked across machine learning, computational neuroscience, and computer vision. At the A\*STAR Institute of High Performance Computing (A\*STAR IHPC), I was a research engineer focused on AI for healthcare and FinTech. Earlier, I was a member of the [Learning, Intelligence and Signal Processing (LISP)](https://sites.dartmouth.edu/lisplab/) group at Boston University, advised by [Prof. Peter Chin](https://sites.dartmouth.edu/lisplab/sang-peter-chin/). I earned a master’s degree in Neuroscience from MIT, where I worked in the [Synthetic Neurobiology Group](http://syntheticneurobiology.org/) led by [Prof. Ed Boyden](http://syntheticneurobiology.org/people/display/71/11), and a bachelor’s degree in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from Nanyang Technological University.
